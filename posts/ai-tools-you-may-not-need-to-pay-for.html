10 AI tools you may not need to pay for—and what to use instead

Start with the free tier or a cheaper plan. Upgrade when a specific feature, limit or license justifies the cost.

By Yared · Prices and plan details checked October 6, 2026

A subscription can be excellent and still be unnecessary for your workload. The clearest reasons to delay payment are a sufficient free tier, a paid tier you rarely use, or a cheaper plan that already covers the feature you need.

This guide is for individuals and small teams making light or occasional use of these products. The order follows workflows, not a quality ranking.

How we researched: We checked official pricing, plan comparisons and help documentation on October 6, 2026. Vendor facts are separated from our conditional recommendations. This is a documented plan comparison, not hands-on performance testing or proof that free tools produce identical results.

The quick comparison

Prices are in USD before tax. Annual equivalents require an annual commitment; they are not month-to-month offers. Unless stated otherwise, seat-based prices apply per person. Promotions are excluded. Plan names, availability and checkout totals can differ by region.

01 / Writing

Grammarly Pro

$30/member/month; or $144/member/year ($12/month equivalent).

Skip the upgrade when: You mainly need spelling, grammar and occasional short writing help.

Use first: Grammarly Free.

Verified plan facts: Free includes basic correctness checks, tone visibility and 100 AI prompts per month. Pro adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustments and 2,000 AI prompts per member per month.

Limits that matter: Free does not include the full set of advanced editing suggestions. Plan names and local prices can differ by country.

Pay when: You write frequently across apps and repeatedly use advanced suggestions or one-click rewrites. The integrated workflow may be worth more than a separate copy-and-paste tool.

Official sources: Plans and free features · US Pro billing

02 / Rewriting

QuillBot Premium

$19.95/month; or $99.95/year (advertised as $8.33/month equivalent).

Skip the upgrade when: You occasionally rephrase a paragraph and do not need a continuous long-document workflow.

Use first: QuillBot Free.

Verified plan facts: The free product provides basic grammar checking and two paraphrasing modes. Premium adds broader paraphrasing access, advanced recommendations and additional writing tools.

Limits that matter: The live pricing page retrieved for this edition showed a 100-word free paraphrasing cap, while another official indexed version showed 125 words. We therefore describe it as a short-text cap; check your actual interface. Limits can vary by platform.

Pay when: You regularly work with longer text or need the additional modes and advanced tools. Paying for convenience is reasonable; buying another rewriter solely for occasional use is harder to justify.

Official sources: Plans and limits · Monthly and annual billing

03 / Workspace

Notion Business for AI

$20/member/month equivalent when paid annually ($240/member/year). Check the monthly billing option separately.

Skip the upgrade when: You are considering a workspace upgrade mainly to summarize a few personal notes.

Use first: Notion Free for organizing; manual summaries or an assistant you already have for occasional drafting.

Verified plan facts: Ongoing Notion AI access is included in Business and Enterprise. Free and Plus provide limited trial AI responses; upgrading to Plus does not renew those responses.

Limits that matter: Notion Free is an alternative for organization, not an unlimited substitute for Notion AI. An external assistant does not reproduce connected workspace search, permissions or automation. Premium models and Custom Agents can require additional credits.

Pay when: You regularly need AI inside shared workspace context, meeting notes or connected search, or you also need the Business collaboration and administration features. Evaluate the whole bundle.

Official sources: AI access and allowances · Plan-upgrade rules · Annual Business rate

04 / Meetings

Otter Pro

$16.99/user/month; the main pricing page lists $8.49/user/month equivalent with annual billing.

Skip the upgrade when: You need only a few short transcriptions and can work within Basic’s restrictions.

Use first: Otter Basic.

Verified plan facts: Basic includes 300 transcription minutes per month, a 30-minute limit per transcription and three lifetime file imports. Pro expands recording and import allowances and adds advanced export and search features.

Limits that matter: Three imports means three per account, not three every month. A one-hour meeting is not fully covered by a 30-minute transcription limit. Some official landing pages still advertise $8.33; we use the main pricing page’s current $8.49 annual-equivalent rate.

Pay when: Your meetings run longer, you regularly import recordings, or you need the paid export and history workflow.

Official sources: Current main pricing · Basic restrictions

05 / Meetings

Fireflies Pro

$18/seat/month; or $10/seat/month equivalent billed annually ($120/seat/year).

Skip the upgrade when: You are trying meeting capture and do not yet need a large searchable archive or regular downloads.

Use first: Fireflies Free.

Verified plan facts: Free includes limited AI summaries and 400 minutes of storage per team. Pro includes broader storage, unlimited AI summaries and downloads of transcripts, summaries and recordings.

Limits that matter: Storage minutes are not a monthly transcription allowance. The advertised free transcription offer has conditions; do not read it as unrestricted storage, summaries or exports. Additional AI-credit features may cost extra beyond the seat subscription.

Pay when: You need regular transcript downloads, a larger retained archive or the paid workflow features. Avoid paying for two notetakers unless each solves a distinct need.

Official sources: Feature comparison · Billing and AI credits

06 / Presentations

Gamma Plus

$108/seat/year ($9/seat/month equivalent with annual billing). Month-to-month pricing is a separate option.

Skip the upgrade when: You want to try AI slide generation or make a one-off deck and can accept Gamma branding.

Use first: Gamma Free for an initial project.

Verified plan facts: Free starts with 400 AI credits and allows up to 10 slides per prompt. It supports exports including PDF and PPTX. Plus supplies 1,000 monthly credits and removes Gamma branding.

Limits that matter: The 400 free credits do not refill. Free is useful for an initial project, not an endlessly renewable AI presentation subscription. Credit consumption depends on the action and model.

Pay when: You create decks repeatedly, need recurring credits or need branding removed from shared and exported work.

Official sources: Plans and branding · Free and paid credit behavior · Credit rules

07 / Research

Perplexity Pro

$20/month; or $200/year ($16.67/month equivalent, rounded).

Skip the upgrade when: You mostly ask occasional, straightforward questions and are not regularly hitting research or upload limits.

Use first: Perplexity Standard.

Verified plan facts: Standard provides basic search with limited advanced searches and file uploads. Pro adds advanced-model access, expanded research and higher usage limits.

Limits that matter: The free tier does not provide the full Pro research, model-selection or file-analysis workflow. Exact quotas can change; a cited answer still needs its sources checked.

Pay when: Frequent research, advanced models or document analysis make the expanded access useful to your actual work. Check for overlap with tools you already use before adding a subscription.

Official sources: Current plans and free limits · US monthly and annual price FAQ

08 / Design

Canva Pro

The US pricing page lists $144/person/year ($12/month equivalent). Check month-to-month pricing at checkout.

Skip the upgrade when: You create occasional graphics with free assets and do not require paid editing, scheduling or brand tools.

Use first: Canva Free.

Verified plan facts: Free provides design templates, free media and limited AI access. The current comparison describes a pooled monthly allowance of up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses, depending on tools and task complexity. Pro adds premium assets, background removal, brand features and more AI access.

Limits that matter: Free assets and AI tools are not the same as Pro’s library or editing features. The advertised AI-use counts are estimates from a shared allowance, not guaranteed separate quotas for every tool.

Pay when: You repeatedly need premium assets, background removal, social scheduling or the extra design and AI capacity. Canva is a design bundle, so judge more than its text generation.

Official sources: US price, features and AI allowance

09 / Voice

ElevenLabs Creator

Creator: $22/month at the standard rate. Starter: $6/month. First-month promotions are excluded from this comparison.

Skip the upgrade when: You are experimenting with voices or do not need Creator’s capacity and professional voice cloning.

Use first: ElevenLabs Free for non-commercial testing; Starter for modest commercial needs.

Verified plan facts: Free includes 10,000 credits per month. Starter includes 30,000, commercial licensing and instant voice cloning; Creator includes 121,000 and professional voice cloning. Credits are shared across products.

Limits that matter: Free-generated content cannot be used commercially and requires attribution when published non-commercially. Paying later does not retroactively license free-generated audio. Commercial use of paid output remains subject to applicable terms; beta services are excluded.

Pay when: Creator’s larger allowance or professional voice cloning is necessary for your production. For a small commercial workload, first check whether Starter is enough.

Official sources: Current standard prices and credits · Publishing and commercial-use rules

10 / Coding

GitHub Copilot Pro+

Pro+: $39/user/month. Pro: $10/user/month.

Skip the upgrade when: You mainly want code completions and occasional help, rather than sustained premium-model or agent usage.

Use first: Copilot Free for light use; Pro if you need unlimited completions.

Verified plan facts: Free provides 2,000 code completions per month. Pro already includes unlimited code completions and next-edit suggestions. Pro+ expands premium-model access and the included AI-credit allowance.

Limits that matter: Unlimited completions does not mean unlimited agent or chat usage. Current billing meters model-backed work in AI credits; additional usage can cost extra. Older premium-request tables should not be treated as the current billing system.

Pay when: You routinely need the premium model selection or higher included usage and have checked actual consumption. A higher tier makes sense when it removes a recurring constraint.

Official sources: Current plans · Current AI-credit billing

A simple subscription rule

Before upgrading, name the exact limitation you are hitting. Keep a short record of how often it interrupts you and whether the paid feature actually improves the final result. Start with the lowest plan that solves that problem.

An annual discount only saves money if you need the product throughout the commitment. For a single project, compare the total annual charge with the cost of a month-to-month plan.

What this comparison cannot establish

Published features do not establish output quality, accuracy or suitability for every workflow. Copy-and-paste alternatives cannot automatically replace integration, collaboration or administration features. An employer-provided plan may also change the decision.

Free quotas, credit systems, regional pricing and product bundles can change. Two official pages sometimes disagree, as the QuillBot and Otter entries show. We flag those discrepancies instead of silently selecting the more attractive figure.

For workplace material, use tools and recording workflows your organization permits. For commercial content, check the applicable license. These requirements may make a paid plan worthwhile even when a free quota would be sufficient.

Our read

Pay for a recurring need you can identify. Try the free option, understand its limits and upgrade when the paid feature earns its place in your workflow.
