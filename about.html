ABOUT

Evidence first. Human agency always.

The Neural Tide is an independent research project about what artificial intelligence can actually do, what remains uncertain, and how people can prepare without surrendering judgment, creativity or agency.

Our goal is not to predict one inevitable future.

We investigate narrow questions, use current sources, separate measured evidence from interpretation, map plausible scenarios, and turn the result into practical preparation.

What we cover

Our core subjects are AI capability, work and careers, autonomous agents, security and risk, social adaptation, opportunity, and the changing role of human contribution.

What makes the project different

We avoid treating forecasts as facts. Articles deliberately distinguish Known, Uncertain, and Speculation. When we give an interpretation, we label it as our read rather than presenting it as measured evidence.

How we use AI

AI tools may assist with research organization, drafting, coding, data presentation and editing. The published work is intended to be source-driven and human-directed: topics, claims, framing, scenario logic and final publication decisions are reviewed rather than generated and posted blindly.

Corrections

AI evidence changes quickly. When stronger evidence appears, we may revise an article, clarify a limitation or change our interpretation. Material updates should be reflected in the article's date and wording.
