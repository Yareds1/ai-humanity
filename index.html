THE QUESTION

Robots can perform much of physical work. Why aren't they replacing workers?

New research estimates robots can technically perform 74% of U.S. physical job tasks in some settings—but are cost-competitive for only 0.3% of work.

Our method: Known = reported evidence. Uncertain = limits/open questions. Speculation = plausible implications, not forecasts.

Anthropic estimates robots can perform 74% of physical tasks in at least some settings, equal to about 34% of all work time.
