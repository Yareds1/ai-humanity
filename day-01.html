Could AI actually cause human extinction?

This is our first question. We are not starting with a conclusion. We are starting with evidence.

The question

Could a future AI system become capable enough, autonomous enough, or misaligned enough to create a catastrophic outcome for humanity?

What we need to investigate

There are several separate claims hidden inside the phrase AI could end humanity. We need to distinguish current AI risks from hypothetical future risks, technical failure modes from misuse, and expert forecasts from speculation.

  • How capable are frontier AI systems today?
  • What mechanisms could produce loss of human control?
  • How seriously do AI researchers take extinction risk?
  • What evidence supports or challenges those concerns?
  • Which safety and governance measures could reduce the risk?
